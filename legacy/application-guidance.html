APPLICATION GUIDANCE

Apply with an organised, complete application.

Use this checklist to prepare before scholarship deadlines close.

01

Read the official eligibility criteria

Confirm the course, category, income, marks, and location requirements.

02

Prepare required documents

Keep identity proof, academic records, income or category certificates, admission documents, and bank details ready.

03

Write and review your supporting statement

Follow the exact prompt and use the SOP guidance to make your answer specific.

04

Submit before the deadline

Allow time for uploads, verification, and corrections. Save your acknowledgement after submission.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Missing a mandatory document or using an expired certificate.
  • Applying without checking the official deadline and renewal conditions.
  • Submitting information that differs across forms and documents.
