COMMON QUESTIONS

Scholarship FAQ

Can I apply for more than one scholarship?

Often, yes—but each provider has its own rules. Check whether awards can be combined and whether accepting one affects another application.

When should I start looking for scholarships?

Start early, ideally before admission or well ahead of the academic year. This gives you time to gather documents and meet provider deadlines.

Do scholarships always require excellent marks?

No. Some providers consider financial need, course, community, gender, location, leadership, sport or other criteria alongside academic performance.

Where should I submit my scholarship application?

Always follow the provider’s official application instructions and portal. Do not rely on third-party information alone for a final submission.
