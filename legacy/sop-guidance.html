SOP GUIDANCE

Tell your story with clarity and purpose.

A strong Statement of Purpose connects your academic background, motivation, goals, and the scholarship’s purpose in your own voice.

A simple SOP structure

  1. Open with your academic motivation.
  2. Show relevant experiences and achievements.
  3. Explain your course and future goals.
  4. Connect those goals to the scholarship.

Useful tips

  • Be specific, concise, and truthful.
  • Use examples instead of broad claims.
  • Tailor each SOP to the scholarship.
  • Proofread for clarity and consistency.

Do

Use a clear structure, answer the prompt directly, and show how funding will support a credible study plan.

Don’t

Reuse generic text, exaggerate achievements, or submit without checking the scholarship’s exact requirements.
