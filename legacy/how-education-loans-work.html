THE BIG PICTURE

A loan is one part of your study plan—not the whole story.

Education loans can help fund eligible study costs. The right route depends on your destination, course, university, costs, co-applicant profile, and the lender's assessment.

PLAIN LANGUAGE, PLEASE

Key terms made simple

Open a term when you need a quick, jargon-free explanation.

Secured vs unsecured loan

A secured loan may use acceptable collateral. An unsecured loan does not, but eligibility and pricing may differ.

Collateral

An asset a lender may accept as security for a loan, such as eligible property or a fixed deposit.

Co-applicant

A person who applies with you—often a parent or guardian—and whose financial profile may be assessed.

Moratorium period

A period when regular EMI payments may be deferred, subject to the lender's policy and interest terms.

Interest rate

The cost charged by the lender for borrowing. Always check whether the rate is fixed or can change.

EMI

Your Equated Monthly Instalment: the regular amount paid toward the loan after repayment begins.

Loan sanction letter

A lender document that sets out its approval, amount, and important conditions before disbursement.
